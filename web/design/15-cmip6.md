## 1. The place chart `#place-chart`
```
.chead   h2 "{name} at {pin.label}, {Y(first month)} to 2100"                          button "Download CSV"
         p  "{each} Records: <a record_url>{src} ↗</a>. {latest.text}. Future: <a p.source.record_url>IPCC AR6 Atlas, CMIP6 ↗</a> for {cellShort}."
.scen    fieldset "Emissions scenario" (4 radios, as sea level; no checkbox)
p        "Scenarios are possible paths for the world's emissions. Experts give no odds for which one will happen."
p#future-read · p.fsub · [p.flag cell] · [p.flag records vs models]
svg · .legend (3 rows) · p.note (gap note) · p.readout · p.summary · details "Show the data as a table" · details "Show the projections as a table"
```
1. **Notation.** `p` = `t.projection`; windows `w` = 0, 1, 2 (2021–2040, 2041–2060, 2081–2100), `{W}` = "{start}–{end}". `v(x)`: 2 significant figures, half away from zero, `+` or true minus; °C after a no-break space ("+1.9 °C", "+0.55 °C"), rain "%" with no space ("+11%", "−2.9%"); Units imperial: × 1.8, then rounded, "°F" ("+3.4 °F"); rain stays %. `rng(a, b)` = the page's `spanText`: both ends to the same decimals, enough that they differ, as the server's `readouts.by_2050.text` and `headline_rate.range_text` ("+1.2 to +2.3 °C", "+0.5 to +1.8 °C", "−3 to +24%"). `{N}` = `S.models[1]`. `{agree%}` = round(`S.agree[w]` × 100). `{each}`: temperature and hottest day "Each dot is one year's average, compared with {baseline.text}."; rain PR1's "Each dot is one year's rain, compared with {baseline.text}." `{cellShort}`: land cell "the 1° grid cell around it"; nearest land cell "the nearest land grid cell"; mostly ocean "the 1° grid cell here". `{cell}`: land cell "the 1° grid cell around this place (its centre is {km} km away)"; nearest land cell "the nearest land grid cell (its centre is {km} km away)"; mostly ocean "the 1° grid cell around this place, which is mostly ocean". `{what}`: air temperature "It is the change in the year's average temperature, not in summer alone."; hottest day "It is the change in each month's hottest day, averaged over the year, not in the year's single hottest day."; rain "It is the change in the year's total rain, not in heavy downpours."
2. **Readout line** (17 px): "<b>{v(S.p50[1])} around 2050</b> under {S.name} ({S.code}): the 2041–2060 average{ yearly rain}, with a range across climate models (10th–90th percentile) of {rng(S.p10[1], S.p90[1])}." Then temperature and hottest day "That is {more}." (only with `now`); rain with `S.disagree[1]` "Models disagree on wetter or drier: {agree%} agree on the direction, under the 80% the IPCC Atlas asks for." `{more}` = `readouts.more_than_today.text` for the default scenario; else `S.p50[1] − now.value` → "about {v unsigned} more than today" ("less"; "about the same as today" at 0; "than in {year}" as the server words it for an old record). Sub-line (14 px grey): "From {N} climate models, for {cell}. {p.caveat} {what}"
3. **Flags** (`.flag`, 14 px): nearest land cell or mostly ocean: `location.note` verbatim. Records vs models (verdict rising or falling, and `S.p50[1]` of the other sign, and not `S.disagree[1]`): "Here the records show {measure} {rising|falling}, while the climate models project it {falling|rising} by 2041–2060. The records describe this place since {Y(fit.start)}; the models give a 20-year average for a grid cell about 100 km wide under one emissions path. We show both as they are." (`{measure}` from 10 §2; rain "rain", "less rain", "more rain".)
4. **Box**: drawn at its width, height 400, margins left 52, right 170 (96 under 600 px), top 40, bottom 34. x from the first January of the record to 1 January 2101; ticks by PR1's spacing rule (every 20 years at 1440, 40 at 375). y covers the yearly values, 0, the trend's 95% range at both ends, and p10 to p90 of all four scenarios in all three windows, so switching scenarios never moves it. y title as PR1 ("°C compared with the 1995–2014 average"; rain "Each year's rain, % above or below the 1995–2014 average"; narrow titles as PR1).
5. **How a window is drawn: a box across its 20 years.** x from 1 January of `start` to 1 January of `end + 1`; y from `p10` (bottom) to `p90` (top). Why: each value is a 20-year average, so it is drawn across those 20 years. A point with a bar at 2050 would read as one year; a band joining the windows would invent 2061–2080 and an annual path the data don't have. Fill `--scen-k` of `S` at `--scen-band`; a 1 px edge in the same colour at full strength, inset 0.5 px. `S`'s median: a 3 px bar across the box, butt ends. The other three scenarios: their medians only, 1.25 px bars across each window, full colour (no boxes). Rain: a window whose `S.disagree[w]` is true has a dashed edge (`4 3`) and a dashed median (`7 4`). Never joined to the observations or to each other (D4); nothing for 2061–2080. A null value: that window's box or bar is not drawn.
6. **Marks, back to front**: baseline years band (PR1, its label at the foot); grid; `S`'s boxes and edges; the trend's 95% range and edges (inside the record only); every casing as one layer (`--surface`, 6 px under `S`'s medians and under the trend line from 1 January 2021 where the first box starts, 4.25 px under the other medians; butt ends); the yearly line (`s-month`, 1 px, joining the dots, broken at missing years) and the zero line; the other medians; `S`'s medians; the trend line (3 px, verdict colour, `fit.start` to `fit.end`, no end dot); the yearly dots (ink, r 3.4; a year needs 9 valid, non-preliminary months, rain uses `t.yearly`); the "Today" rule and its label (sea level); the Now dot and label (sea level: r 5 ink, 2 px ring, "Now {now.text}" 13 px 600, 30 px up and 10 left, end-anchored, 1 px leader; none without `now`); above `S`'s 2041–2060 box, centred, 10 px over its p90: "{v(p50)}, 2041–2060" (13 px 600 ink, 5 px `--surface` halo; under 600 px "{v(p50)}"); rain with that window disagreeing (600 px and up): "Models disagree" (12 px `--ink-2`, halo) 8 px over p90 and the value label 16 px higher.
7. **Right margin**: "2081–2100:" 12 px grey above the top label; per scenario "{short} {v(p50[2])}" 12.5 px, `S` 650 ink, others 400 `--ink-2`; at least 16 px apart, pushed down; a 1 px leader in the scenario colour from the plot's right edge at the median to the label. 8. **Legend** (rows led by grey group words): "From the records:" [line and dot] Each year's average (rain: Each year's rain) · [dot] Now / "Our calculation:" Trend line · 95% range of the trend · 1995–2014 average (zero) / "Climate models (IPCC):" [four stubs] Middle estimate for each scenario, named at the right · [box with bar] Range across climate models (10th–90th percentile), {short lower case} · [dashed box, rain only when a window of `S` disagrees] Dashed: models disagree on wetter or drier · [dashed stub] Today.
9. **Gap note** (14 px grey): "Each box is the climate models' average for 20 years, drawn across those years, so single years will swing above and below it. We add the models' change to this place's own 1995–2014 average and never move it to meet the measured dots, so a gap is real. The IPCC Atlas uses these three periods, so 2061–2080 has no box."
10. **Readout**: hint "Hover, tap or use the arrow keys to read a year, then each 20-year period after the record ends." 11. **Text summary** (visible, and the SVG's `aria-label`): "{name} at {place}, {M(first)} to {M(period.end)}, and climate-model projections for 2021–2040, 2041–2060 and 2081–2100, all compared with {baseline.text}. {PR1's trend sentence} Now: {now.text} ({now.label}). ## 5. States
- **Available, land cell**: as above. **Nearest land cell / mostly ocean**: the sub-line's `{cellShort}` and `{cell}`, the flag, step 7 line 3, the CSV line.
- **Rain, models disagree**: dashed boxes, the chart label (600 px and up), the card sentence, the readout sentence, the table column, step 7's agreement line. Agreement at 80% or more: no sentence anywhere.
- **Records vs models opposite** (e.g. Delhi hottest day: records falling, models +1.3 °C): the flag (§1.3). Nothing else changes; both are drawn.
- **No projection** (`available` false): the record has no 1995–2014 average ("This record has no 1995–2014 average to start the projection from, so we don't show one.", e.g. a station that starts after 2014) or the cell has no value ("The climate models have no value for this place."). The page is PR1 for that measurement: monthly chart, no radios, no boxes; R3 label "What experts project" with `p.why` (14 px grey); step 7 prints `p.why` and PR1's "We never stretch…". Disclaimer: the server's past-only wording.
- **A window with no value**: no box or bar; readout, table "no value"; R3 when 2041–2060 is null: value "—", line "{S.code}, IPCC AR6 Atlas. No value for 2041–2060 here."
- **`now` null**: no Now dot, no more sentence, no Now line in step 7. Old record: "Latest" and "than in {year}" as sea level.
- **Sources disagree** (POWER and a station, the model note): unchanged. Each answer shows its own projection and cell; nothing is merged.
- **Loading, error, no pin**: PR1 (the projection comes in the same answer). Units imperial: °F differences, rain unchanged, CSV metric.
